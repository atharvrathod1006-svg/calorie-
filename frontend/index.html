Scientific Formulas & Methodology

Mifflin-St Jeor Equation

Adopted by the American Dietetic Association as the most clinically accurate formula for healthy adults:

10 × W(kg) + 6.25 × H(cm) - 5 × Age ± Gender Constant

Katch-McArdle Formula

Calculates resting energy based purely on lean body mass (LBM). Ideal for athletes and individuals with measured body fat %.

370 + (21.6 × Lean Body Mass in kg)

Safe Deficit Guidelines

A deficit of 500 kcal/day targets approximately 0.5 kg (1.1 lbs) of fat loss per week while minimizing muscle breakdown and hormonal adaptation.
